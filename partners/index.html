For accountability partners

Someone you know chose you to support them.

Gracia is an app that helps people build habits and a steadier life. When someone adds you as an accountability partner, they are asking for a little encouragement. It is not a burden or a monitoring job.

What you can see

  • The habits they chose to share with you, and whether each day was done, missed or a struggle.
  • That is all. You never see their notes, their journal, their conversations with Gracia, or anything else.
  • You open a private link they send you. You don't need an account or the app. The link is encrypted, and our servers can't read what is on it.

Messages from Gracia on WhatsApp (optional)

If the person turns on automatic alerts, Gracia can message you on WhatsApp when they have missed a habit for several days or are struggling, so you can reach out. For example: "Hi Sam, this is Gracia. Alex asked you to keep them accountable, and they haven't logged Gym in 3 days. A short message from you can make a real difference."

  • You choose. Gracia only messages you after you opt in, by sending the word START to Gracia's WhatsApp number using the link in the invitation.
  • You can stop any time. Reply STOP and you will get no more messages. Any waiting alerts are deleted.
  • Alerts never contain a link or anything beyond the person's first name, the habit and the number of days.
  • Your number is used only to send you these alerts. See the privacy policy for exactly what is kept and for how long.

How to help

A short, kind message is enough: "Thinking of you. How's it going?" Skip the lecture. If someone seems to be in real distress, encourage them to reach out to a professional or a crisis line.

Questions

Email graciaapp.team@gmail.com. To stop messages immediately, reply STOP on WhatsApp.
