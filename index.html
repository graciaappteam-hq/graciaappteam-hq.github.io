An AI life companion

Every person was made for something.

Gracia is an AI companion that walks with you to find it and live it: your habits, your week, your goals, and someone to talk it through with. One intentional day at a time.

In private testing on iPhone. Coming to the App Store.

Everything in one quiet place

Not another dashboard to feel behind on. Gracia keeps the whole picture, and speaks to you like a friend who has it.

Habits and your week

Build habits, see your schedule as a calm timeline, and keep a to-do list sorted by what is urgent and what truly matters.

Goals and identity

Connect each habit to the goal it serves, and write who you are becoming. Gracia asks what a habit is for when it is not tied to anything.

Talk it through

Type, speak, or have a live conversation. Gracia knows your day and your goals, and she can add or change things with your yes.

An evening review

A short conversation about your habits, then a few minutes of journaling. Miss a night and you can catch up on yesterday.

Support when it is hard

Tap play on a habit you are struggling with and Gracia stays with you through the moment. She remembers what helped before.

Accountability, gently

Choose someone you trust to see only done or missed marks. Never your notes, never your journal.

Your life stays on your phone

Your habits, schedule, goals, journal and contacts live on your device. Your journal, including anything you say during the evening journaling, never leaves it. Backups are encrypted before they go anywhere.

When you talk to Gracia, your message and a short summary of your day are sent securely to an AI provider to write her reply. We do not keep them. Read exactly what is shared and what is not.

Faith, only if you want it

Gracia is open to everyone. She grew out of a Catholic worldview: prayer, the Examen, a life ordered toward what matters. She only brings prayer and Scripture into a conversation if you welcome it. For people of other faiths, or none, she reflects back your own practices and never writes them for you.

Gracia is a companion, not a therapist, doctor or priest. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line.
