Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version

Your data lives on your device. When you talk to Gracia, your message and a short snapshot of your life are sent through our server to an AI provider to write a reply; we don't keep them. Your journal stays on your phone. Only if you switch it on in Health, short excerpts of your last few Gratitude, Reflection and Tomorrow entries go into your chat with Gracia; the Examen never does. Backups are encrypted on your phone; only your Gracia password can open them on another phone.

Data we store

On our servers: very little. No habits, goals, schedule, journal, conversations, finance or contacts are stored on our servers. All of that lives in a local database on your device. What our servers do keep:

  • Your sign-in account (email) with Supabase, our authentication provider. No user content is stored there.
  • Anonymous usage counts to run and fund the service: a one-way hash of your account id (the raw id is not stored), plus counts such as how many messages you sent and how many AI tokens were used per month, and counters like "onboarding completed". It cannot contain message text. The app can show you your own usage for the month, and only you can see it.
  • Accountability relay data, only if you add an accountability partner: an encrypted blob we cannot read.
  • Automatic WhatsApp alert records, only if you add an accountability partner with alert thresholds, automatic alerts are switched on, and the partner has opted in. This is a small temporary record holding the partner's phone number and first name, your first name, the habit title, a day count and the time to send. It is deleted when the alert is sent, when it is replaced (you logged the habit again, removed the partner, or deleted the habit) or when it goes stale. After sending, only a bare "already sent" marker with no names, number or habit is kept for up to 60 days so the same alert is not sent twice. It never contains your notes, your habit history or the encrypted share key. When a partner uses a WhatsApp username, WhatsApp gives us an anonymous business-scoped user ID for them instead of a phone number; if so, we keep that ID (and a short code linking it to the partner) in the same temporary way and under the same rules.

Data that passes through our servers

When you chat with Gracia (in the Gracia tab, the quick-chat button, Talk me through it, the evening review call, onboarding or reviews), the app sends your message and a short snapshot of context to our server, which forwards it to Anthropic PBC, our AI provider, to generate a reply. We do not store it. Anthropic processes it under its own terms and retention policy.

The snapshot can include: your name, age, location, living situation and family details, faith background and how you'd like faith language handled, how you'd like Gracia to talk to you (gentle or direct), today's habits and streaks, how long you spent on each habit, short notes you gave about why a habit slipped, your goals, schedule and identity statements, things you're working to stop or cut back on (as habit names), the names of a few connections due a catch-up, your savings, giving and runway percentages (never raw amounts), and short "memories" Gracia keeps about you. Each message is also checked by a separate automated safety classifier (also Anthropic) so Gracia can respond to signs of crisis.

More of your day. The snapshot also includes your weekly schedule, each habit’s title with its days, time of day and usual length (and the name of a place you tagged for it), the titles of your to-dos that are overdue or due in the next week, and the first names of people your weekly plan puts on today. Your recent mood word (never your note) goes too, unless you switch it off under “What Gracia sees”. Optional and off by default: your weight, height and BMI (adults only).

Slips on a habit you are stopping. If you tell Gracia about a slip (when it happened, whether you were alone, how you felt, or what helped on a day you held), the details are kept on your device as small tags, and a few words for what helped. After a few slips, Gracia is sent only a short summary, such as “4 of the last 5 slips were late at night, 3 when alone”, plus a couple of the phrases you said helped. What you said about each slip is not sent as such.

Trips. Trips you tell Gracia about (place, dates, purpose) stay on your phone; only a one-line summary goes with your messages so she can adjust to them.

Different days. Days you tell Gracia are different from your usual plan (a title, dates and which habits they skip) stay on your phone; only a one-line summary of today and tomorrow goes with your messages, never your reason, and nothing about them reaches your accountability partners.

Location. If you turn on location, your home and the places you tag stay on your phone, and visits are deleted after 30 days. Gracia gets only a rounded distance from home while you're away and the names and times of tagged places you visited today, never coordinates, and the app never sends your location to anyone, us included.

Time out of home. If you switch on “Notice when I leave and come home”, Gracia sees only the times you were out of home and how many days you have stayed in, never where you went, and those times are deleted after 30 days too.

Health profile. Your health profile (conditions, medicines, injuries, allergies, doctor's advice, results and visits) stays on your phone. Only if you turn on Health profile under "What Gracia sees" (Health → Profile) does a short summary go with your messages, never your visit notes.

A health document you scan. The picture is sent once to be read, then thrown away by our server. The copy you keep stays on this phone and isn't in your backups.

Body breakdown screenshots. Screenshots you choose are sent once to read the numbers and are deleted; Gracia's server keeps nothing. The numbers stay on this phone, and Gracia sees them only with the Weight switch on.

Food log. Your food log stays on your phone. Searching foods happens on your phone. When you describe or photograph a meal, the text or photo (with its location and camera details removed) is sent to our server to estimate it and is not stored, on our server or in Gracia. Only if you turn on Food under "What Gracia sees" (Health → Profile) do your daily totals go with your messages, never what you ate.

Amounts on a habit you are stopping. For a habit you are stopping or cutting back on, Gracia sees how many days you held or went over in the last 7 days, never your notes, reasons or the time of day. Your accountability partner sees held or slipped only; they see an amount, like “3 drinks”, only if you turn on sharing amounts for that partner.

Your training plan. Your training plan stays on your phone. Gracia sees its shape (days and their names, length, equipment); she sees which joints it avoids only if you share your health profile. Photos of your gym, if you add them, are sent to our server once to spot the equipment (with their location and camera details removed) and are not stored.

Exercises you look up. When you add an exercise by typing a name that is not in the library or your saved list, Gracia looks it up on the web automatically, and you can also ask her to look one up. Only that name (and which joints to be careful with) is sent to our server and searched on the web. Nothing is stored. Saved exercises stay on your phone. To fit the answer to you, the lookup also sends the equipment you have and whether you are under 18. When a name you type does not match an exercise on your phone, it may also be sent to our server, with the names of the exercises in the library and in your saved list, only to work out which exercise you mean. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is stored. Gracia sees only the names of the exercises you save.

A training plan you bring. Your plan is sent to read it, then thrown away. It isn't saved on our server. The plan Gracia reads from it stays on this phone.

Gracia's workout suggestion. When you ask for Gracia's suggestion, the app sends counts (sets per muscle, sports played, how recent sessions felt), your equipment and exercise names to work out a workout. If you share Apple Health with Gracia, it also sends your recent sport workouts (type, minutes and day), your sleep and your average steps. Gracia may suggest up to two new exercises, which are looked up on the web by name. No notes, weights, places or health details beyond the joints to protect are sent, and nothing is stored.

Your training log. Your sets, reps and weights stay on your phone. Gracia sees how many sessions you did, which exercises are going up or stuck, and how many sets each muscle group got this week, never the weights. Your machine settings stay on this phone.

The science in Talk me through it. In Talk me through it, the science card is looked up on the web: the habit's name, whether you are starting it or stopping it, and, only if you tapped one of the suggested answers, that answer are sent to our server, once a week at most per habit and answer. Words you type yourself are never sent, and nothing is stored.

The line at the top of Talk me through it. At the top of Talk me through it, Gracia writes a short line for you. To do that, your reason for the habit, the “I am someone who…” statements you wrote, a goal title, the habit’s name, whether you are starting or stopping it, the time on your clock, whether you are Catholic or Christian (or neither), and short labels of what helped you before are sent to our server and the AI service; nothing is kept. The line is saved on your phone for the day.

The reflection under the verses. When you're struggling, the habit's name, your tradition (Catholic or Christian) and the verses that helped you before or that Gracia showed you lately are sent so the verses and a short reflection can be chosen and looked up on the web for you; nothing is kept.

What helped in Talk me through it. After Talk me through it, what you tick as having helped stays on your phone. Only a short line of those labels, such as “waiting 10 minutes · 1 Corinthians 10:13”, goes with your messages so Gracia can start with what works for you. To list her key points, Gracia’s own replies from that conversation (never what you wrote) are sent once to be summarised; nothing is kept.

Web search in chat. In chat, Gracia may search the web for a science question. She uses a general phrase, never your name or details, and the search goes through Anthropic's search provider.

Things Gracia looks up on your phone. When you ask Gracia something specific, like how often you did a habit, when you last saw someone or what you lifted, she can look it up on your phone. Only the few lines that answer the question are sent with your message, never whole tables, and nothing is kept. Anything switched off in Health → What Gracia sees stays off. A look-up never reads your Examen, phone numbers, coordinates or finance amounts, and for a habit you are stopping it gives day counts only.

Mood in Apple Health. If you switch it on, your mood level, the feeling words and what had the biggest impact are saved to Apple Health as a State of Mind entry, never your note, and Gracia only writes it; nothing about it goes to our server.

Help improve Gracia. Unless you switch "Help improve Gracia" off in Settings, the app counts which features are used, like "a workout was logged", never what is in them. The counts carry no text or details, and are kept under an anonymous code, not your name or email.

Time log. Your time log stays on your phone. Unless you switch Time off under "What Gracia sees", Gracia sees your daily totals by category, never what you wrote.

Life areas. Your life areas and weekly ratings stay on your phone. Unless you switch it off under "What Gracia sees", Gracia sees one line of counts for the week, like People 3 of 5, never what you wrote.

What Gracia remembers. What Gracia remembers about you is kept on your phone. She saves short notes automatically, and you can see, edit or delete them in Memories, or tell her never to learn a topic. Nothing sensitive is saved automatically, and only the notes you have not hidden go with your messages.

Day patterns. Patterns across your days are worked out on your phone. With Day patterns on (it starts on), Gracia gets one short line of counts, like "evenings went badly after 3 of 4 long days". Slip details, notes and places never leave your phone. Switch it off under "What Gracia sees".

Festivals your friends celebrate. The festivals you note for a friend stay on your phone, with that person. They are never sent to Gracia or to our server.

Time check-ins. Check-ins are reminders on your phone. Your answers stay on this phone with the rest of your time log; Gracia sees only how many you answered unless you share your time. The time you woke up is read from Apple Health on your phone. It goes to Gracia only if you share your Apple Health numbers with her; otherwise it is not sent anywhere.

Apple Health (optional). If you connect Apple Health, Gracia reads your steps, sleep and workouts (read-only, with one exception you switch on yourself: your mood, below) and keeps a daily summary on your device. That stays on your device. Apple Health numbers (under “What Gracia sees” in Health → Profile) start on when you connect Apple Health, for adults. While they are on, a short summary line, a sleep summary (hours against your need, usual wake time; the nights behind it stay on your device), and where there is enough data a couple of patterns in your own data, are added to the snapshot above. You can switch it off or disconnect Apple Health at any time, and disconnecting clears the summaries saved on your device. We do not use health data for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Never included: the Examen and examination content, journal entries (except the short excerpts of recent Gratitude, Reflection, Tomorrow and note entries that you can switch on in Health, off by default), raw finance amounts, and connection phone numbers and notes. The one thing that can bring a few of these lines along is a look-up you ask for (see “Things Gracia looks up on your phone”); it never includes the Examen, phone numbers, raw finance amounts or anything you have switched off.

Sensitive information

Some of what you may share reveals religious belief, personal struggles (for example a habit you want to stop), health-related habits, or family circumstances. You choose what to tell Gracia, and every sensitive question can be skipped. Anything you tell her in conversation passes through our server and Anthropic as described above.

Voice

Speech is transcribed on your device where possible. If your phone can't do that, Apple's own speech service may process the audio under Apple's terms (the app tells you the first time). Your voice is never recorded or kept by Gracia, and audio never goes to our servers. Gracia's spoken replies are generated on your device.

Account

We use Supabase for authentication (email and password). No user content is stored in Supabase.

Backups

Backups are encrypted on your device with AES-256 before leaving it. The backup key is kept in your device's secure storage and also travels inside each backup, locked with a key made from your Gracia password, so you can restore on a new phone by signing in. The app never stores your password itself, and we never receive the backup key or your backups. If you enable Google Drive backup, Google stores only ciphertext. Anyone who has both a backup file and your password could open it, so choose a strong password. If you reset your password, backups made before the reset open with the old password. Backups made before this protection was set up open only on the phone that made them.

Accountability sharing

If you add an accountability partner, encrypted statistics (your name, the habit titles you choose to share, and done, failed or missed marks) are relayed through our server. We store only ciphertext; the decryption key is in the share link and never reaches our server. The partner sees no notes, hours or journal. Messages you send partners via WhatsApp contain the habit name in plain text, and you compose and send these yourself. If automatic alerts are switched on, Gracia can also send a short alert to a partner who has agreed to receive them. Partners opt in by messaging Gracia's WhatsApp number and can reply STOP at any time. See information for accountability partners. You can remove a partner at any time, which deletes the relayed data. If you catch up on yesterday's review, habits you log for yesterday update the marks your partner sees for that day.

Notifications

All reminders, including habit reminders, "coming up" block reminders, the evening call and journaling call, and monthly finance and weekly review nudges, are scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics and do not track what you do in the app. The only product measurement is the anonymous usage counts above. Our server keeps minimal operational logs with no message content, including access counts for accountability share pages (no personal identity; share pages contain only encrypted data we cannot read).

Your rights

Delete your sign-in account any time in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. That deletes the account, turns off your accountability links and removes the usage counts and partner alerts we hold on our servers, and then offers to erase the data on your phone too. Deleting the app also deletes your data on your device. Export your data any time from Settings, Backup. To ask a question or make a request about the small amount we hold on our servers, email graciaapp.team@gmail.com.

Children

Gracia is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app. Questions: graciaapp.team@gmail.com.
