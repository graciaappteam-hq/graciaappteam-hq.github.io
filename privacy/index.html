Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

The short version

Your data lives on your device. When you talk to Gracia, your message and a short snapshot of your life are sent through our server to an AI provider to write a reply; we don't keep them. Your journal stays on your phone. Only if you switch it on in Health, short excerpts of your last few Gratitude, Reflection and Tomorrow entries go into your chat with Gracia; the Examen never does. Backups are encrypted with a key that never leaves your device.

Data we store

On our servers: very little. No habits, goals, schedule, journal, conversations, finance or contacts are stored on our servers. All of that lives in a local database on your device. What our servers do keep:

  • Your sign-in account (email) with Supabase, our authentication provider. No user content is stored there.
  • Anonymous usage counts to run and fund the service: a one-way hash of your account id (the raw id is not stored), plus counts such as how many messages you sent and how many AI tokens were used per month, and counters like "onboarding completed". It cannot contain message text. The app can show you your own usage for the month, and only you can see it.
  • Accountability relay data, only if you add an accountability partner: an encrypted blob we cannot read.
  • Automatic WhatsApp alert records, only if you add an accountability partner with alert thresholds, automatic alerts are switched on, and the partner has opted in. This is a small temporary record holding the partner's phone number and first name, your first name, the habit title, a day count and the time to send. It is deleted when the alert is sent, when it is replaced (you logged the habit again, removed the partner, or deleted the habit) or when it goes stale. After sending, only a bare "already sent" marker with no names, number or habit is kept for up to 60 days so the same alert is not sent twice. It never contains your notes, your habit history or the encrypted share key. When a partner uses a WhatsApp username, WhatsApp gives us an anonymous business-scoped user ID for them instead of a phone number; if so, we keep that ID (and a short code linking it to the partner) in the same temporary way and under the same rules.

Data that passes through our servers

When you chat with Gracia (in the Gracia tab, the quick-chat button, active sessions, the evening review call, onboarding or reviews), the app sends your message and a short snapshot of context to our server, which forwards it to Anthropic PBC, our AI provider, to generate a reply. We do not store it. Anthropic processes it under its own terms and retention policy.

The snapshot can include: your name, age, location, living situation and family details, faith background and how you'd like faith language handled, how you'd like Gracia to talk to you (gentle or direct), today's habits and streaks, how long you spent on each habit, short notes you gave about why a habit slipped, your goals, schedule and identity statements, things you're working to stop or cut back on (as habit names), the names of a few connections due a catch-up, your savings, giving and runway percentages (never raw amounts), and short "memories" Gracia keeps about you. Each message is also checked by a separate automated safety classifier (also Anthropic) so Gracia can respond to signs of crisis.

More of your day. The snapshot also includes your weekly schedule, each habit’s title with its days, time of day and usual length (and the name of a place you tagged for it), the titles of your to-dos that are overdue or due in the next week, and the first names of people your weekly plan puts on today. Optional and off by default: your recent mood word (never your note) and your weight, height and BMI (adults only).

Slips on a habit you are stopping. If you tell Gracia about a slip (when it happened, whether you were alone, how you felt, or what helped on a day you held), the details are kept on your device as small tags, and a few words for what helped. After a few slips, Gracia is sent only a short summary, such as “4 of the last 5 slips were late at night, 3 when alone”, plus a couple of the phrases you said helped. What you said about each slip is not sent as such.

Trips. Trips you tell Gracia about (place, dates, purpose) stay on your phone; only a one-line summary goes with your messages so she can adjust to them.

Location. If you turn on location, your home and the places you tag stay on your phone, and visits are deleted after 7 days. Gracia gets only a rounded distance from home while you're away and the names and times of tagged places you visited today, never coordinates, and the app never sends your location to anyone, us included.

Health profile. Your health profile (conditions, medicines, injuries, allergies, doctor's advice, results and visits) stays on your phone. Only if you turn on Health profile under "What Gracia sees" (Health → Profile) does a short summary go with your messages, never your visit notes.

Food log. Your food log stays on your phone. Searching foods happens on your phone. When you describe or photograph a meal, the text or photo (with its location and camera details removed) is sent to our server to estimate it and is not stored, on our server or in Gracia. Only if you turn on Food under "What Gracia sees" (Health → Profile) do your daily totals go with your messages, never what you ate.

Amounts on a habit you are stopping. For a habit you are stopping or cutting back on, Gracia sees how many days you held or went over in the last 7 days, never your notes, reasons or the time of day. Your accountability partner sees held or slipped only; they see an amount, like “3 drinks”, only if you turn on sharing amounts for that partner.

Your training plan. Your training plan stays on your phone. Gracia sees its shape (days and their names, length, equipment); she sees which joints it avoids only if you share your health profile. Photos of your gym, if you add them, are sent to our server once to spot the equipment (with their location and camera details removed) and are not stored.

Exercises you look up. If you ask Gracia to look up an exercise, only its name (and which joints to be careful with) is sent to our server and searched on the web. Nothing is stored. Saved exercises stay on your phone. To fit the answer to you, the lookup also sends the equipment you have and whether you are under 18. Gracia sees only the names of the exercises you save.

A training plan you bring. Your plan is sent to read it, then thrown away. It isn't saved on our server. The plan Gracia reads from it stays on this phone.

Your training log. Your sets, reps and weights stay on your phone. Gracia sees how many sessions you did and which exercises are going up or stuck, never the weights.

Time log. Your time log stays on your phone. Only if you turn on Time does Gracia see your daily totals by category, never what you wrote.

Time check-ins. Check-ins are reminders on your phone. Your answers stay on this phone with the rest of your time log; Gracia sees only how many you answered unless you share your time. The time you woke up is read from Apple Health on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

Apple Health (optional). If you connect Apple Health, Gracia reads your steps, sleep and workouts (read-only; it never writes to Apple Health) and keeps a daily summary on your device. That stays on your device. Only if you separately switch on Apple Health numbers under “What Gracia sees” (Health → Profile) is a short summary line, and where there is enough data a couple of patterns in your own data, added to the snapshot above. That is off by default; you can switch it off or disconnect Apple Health at any time, and disconnecting clears the summaries saved on your device. We do not use health data for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Never included: the Examen and examination content, journal entries (except the short excerpts of recent Gratitude, Reflection, Tomorrow and note entries that you can switch on in Health, off by default), raw finance amounts, and connection phone numbers and notes.

Sensitive information

Some of what you may share reveals religious belief, personal struggles (for example a habit you want to stop), health-related habits, or family circumstances. You choose what to tell Gracia, and every sensitive question can be skipped. Anything you tell her in conversation passes through our server and Anthropic as described above.

Voice

Speech is transcribed on your device where possible. If your phone can't do that, Apple's own speech service may process the audio under Apple's terms (the app tells you the first time). Your voice is never recorded or kept by Gracia, and audio never goes to our servers. Gracia's spoken replies are generated on your device.

Account

We use Supabase for authentication (email and password). No user content is stored in Supabase.

Backups

Backups are encrypted on your device with AES-256 before leaving it. The encryption key is stored only in your device's secure storage. If you enable Google Drive backup, Google stores only ciphertext. If you lose your device and your key, we cannot recover your data. A backup can be restored when you set up Gracia on a new phone.

Accountability sharing

If you add an accountability partner, encrypted statistics (your name, the habit titles you choose to share, and done, failed or missed marks) are relayed through our server. We store only ciphertext; the decryption key is in the share link and never reaches our server. The partner sees no notes, hours or journal. Messages you send partners via WhatsApp contain the habit name in plain text, and you compose and send these yourself. If automatic alerts are switched on, Gracia can also send a short alert to a partner who has agreed to receive them. Partners opt in by messaging Gracia's WhatsApp number and can reply STOP at any time. See information for accountability partners. You can remove a partner at any time, which deletes the relayed data. If you catch up on yesterday's review, habits you log for yesterday update the marks your partner sees for that day.

Notifications

All reminders, including habit reminders, "coming up" block reminders, the evening call and journaling call, and monthly finance and weekly review nudges, are scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics and do not track what you do in the app. The only product measurement is the anonymous usage counts above. Our server keeps minimal operational logs with no message content, including access counts for accountability share pages (no personal identity; share pages contain only encrypted data we cannot read).

Your rights

Delete the app to delete your data on your device. Export your data any time from Settings, Backup. To remove your sign-in account, or to ask a question or make a request about the small amount we hold on our servers (your account, hashed usage counts, relay data), email graciaapp.team@gmail.com.

Children

Gracia is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app. Questions: graciaapp.team@gmail.com.
